🚢 Are Viking ships sailing or busing right now?

Answer: both. Viking is still operating cruises, but current low water is causing a mix of normal sailing, motorcoach transfers, hotel substitutions and identical sister-ship swaps on affected Rhine/Danube itineraries.

What this means for our November cruise: there is real operational disruption in the Viking system now, not just theoretical low-water risk. But it is too early to project today's exact bus/swap pattern onto our November 3 sailing.
