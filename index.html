🚢 Are Viking ships sailing or busing right now?

Answer: both. Viking is still operating cruises, but current low water is causing a mix of normal sailing, motorcoach transfers, hotel substitutions and identical sister-ship swaps on affected Rhine/Danube itineraries.

What this means for our November cruise: there is real operational disruption in the Viking system now, not just theoretical low-water risk. But it is too early to project today's exact bus/swap pattern onto our November 3 sailing. The most important indicators will be Kaub, Pfelling/Passau, the Austrian Danube and Viking's departure-specific notices during the final 10–14 days.

🌧️ Weather & River Trend

Kaub remains exceptionally low. Pfelling improved slightly late Sep. 27. Current conditions can change materially after sustained basin-wide rain, so the trend in the final 10–14 days before departure will matter much more than today’s number.

ℹ️ What We're Watching

River cruise ships require adequate water beneath the hull but can also encounter problems when water becomes too high to pass safely beneath bridges.
